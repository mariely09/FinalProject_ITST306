About Cor'z Eatery

Cor'z Eatery is a humble dining spot in Majayjay, Laguna — the perfect stop-over for tourists and motorists heading to or from Sprite Falls. We offer delicious and affordable meals, and we are known among locals and visitors alike for our friendly service and relaxing atmosphere.

About Us

Get to know Cor'z Eatery

Our Story

Cor'z Eatery started as a small dining spot serving the local community of Majayjay, Laguna. Over time, it became a go-to destination for tourists and motorists visiting the famous Sprite Falls, known for its home-cooked style food and excellent service.

Our main goal is to serve every guest with delicious, fresh, and affordable food they will always remember.
